Use what you have.

At sea, Ellen MacArthur learned to work with the supplies she had. Her observation connects to SDG 12, Responsible Consumption and Production, because repairing an object keeps materials in use and can prevent waste. In this textile, each repair spends the thread its tear leaves behind. Before you replace something this week, try one repair with a material you already have and show someone how you did it.
